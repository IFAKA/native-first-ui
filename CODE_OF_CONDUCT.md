# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing impact.
- Welcome accessible and inclusive design perspectives.
- Respect differing opinions and lived experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized language, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's configured contact or security channel. Reports will be handled respectfully and as confidentially as possible.
